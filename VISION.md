# Vision

Brundlefly is a collection of self-contained skills for agents.
The mascot is a human-fly hybrid caught mid-mutation.

Writing gives one person a way to reach another.
Brundlefly aims to preserve meaning, voice, and care for the reader when agents change that writing.
Its brand holds human connection and empathy beside the inhuman, slop, and the grotesque.
These are the purpose and the tension, not claims that agents feel or that a skill guarantees connection.

Each skill must work without personal skills, private services, or machine-specific paths.
Instructions preserve user intent and make required dependencies clear.

Branding can be grotesque and playful.
Task instructions stay clear and practical.
Reject decorative prose that obscures a procedure.
Do not claim that writing edits can guarantee authorship or defeat detection.
