# Vision

Brundlefly is a collection of self-contained skills for transforming text with agents.
The mascot is a human-fly hybrid caught mid-mutation.

Writing gives one person a way to reach another.
Brundlefly aims to preserve meaning, voice, and care for the reader when agents change that writing.
Start with who the human reader is and what they need to understand or do.
Respect them through clear explanations. Optimize for clarity, accuracy, and brevity without losing necessary meaning.
Keep ADHD and dyslexia explicit when designing for varied reading, attention, and working memory needs.
Storytelling helps communicate meaning and supports understanding and connection with a piece of text.
Storytelling, style techniques, and every extra build on respect and empathy for the reader.
Keep useful techniques. Simplify overlapping instructions before removing capabilities.
Its brand holds human connection and empathy beside the inhuman, slop, and the grotesque.
These are the purpose and the tension, not claims that agents feel or that a skill guarantees connection.

Keep the collection focused on text: writing, editing, compression, naming, and consistent wording.
Operational workflows, service management, and deployment policy belong to their owning projects.
Each Skill owns its requested artifact or edit. Installing a Skill does not create root documents.

Each skill must work without personal skills, private services, or machine-specific paths.
Instructions preserve user intent and make required dependencies clear.

Branding can be grotesque and playful.
Task instructions stay clear and practical.
Reject decorative prose that obscures a procedure.
Do not claim that writing edits can guarantee authorship or defeat detection.
